How it's made

From box to bowl in under five minutes

No stove, no oil splatter — just the parts of pani puri that are actually fun.

01

Mix the sour water

Empty one khatta pani sachet into 700 ml cold water and stir.

02

Adjust the heat

Stir in a little hari chutney for extra sharpness, to taste.

03

Fill each puri

Crack a small hole and spoon in the spicy chickpeas.

04

Sweeten and serve

Finish with meethi chutney, dunk in the khatta pani, and eat immediately.
